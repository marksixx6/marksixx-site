Privacy

Your privacy.

Short and simple.

When you contact us

Messages come to us on WhatsApp. The contact form and the “Let’s talk” buttons open WhatsApp with your message ready, and you decide whether to send it. We see what you send there, like your name, number and message, and we use it only to answer you and work with you. We don’t sell it, and we don’t share it for advertising.

No tracking, no cookies

This site has no ads, no analytics and no tracking pixels, and it doesn’t set cookies. To keep things smooth, your browser remembers two small things during your visit: whether you’ve already seen the intro, and where you scrolled. That stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is cleared when you close the tab.

The “Try it” preview

When you add your logo to the preview, it stays on your phone or computer. It’s never uploaded to us. Only what you choose to send us on WhatsApp reaches us.

Other sites

Links to WhatsApp, Instagram and the articles we quote take you to those sites, which have their own privacy rules. Like any website, our web host may keep basic technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) to keep the site running and secure.
