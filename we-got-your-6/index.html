Always. Nothing goes out without your approval, and you can comment on anything in the app.

Very little. A short kickoff, then a few taps a week to approve. That’s the point.

Can I start with one service and grow into this?

Yes. Many clients start with social or a brand refresh and add the rest later.

Who do I talk to?
