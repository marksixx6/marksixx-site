Platforms

The right place for every message.

We don’t do every platform for everyone. We pick the ones your clients actually use, and do them properly.

Instagram

Your storefront on the phone

When someone hears your name, this is usually the first place they look. Your profile has a few seconds to say who you are, show you’re active, and make it easy to reach you.

What we do

  • Profile setup: bio, profile photo, link, contact and WhatsApp buttons
  • Feed posts: designed in your brand so the grid looks like one business
  • Reels: short videos edited for how people actually scroll
  • Stories: the daily, casual updates that keep you in mind
  • Highlights: your reviews, work, FAQ and contact, saved at the top
  • Captions: written in your voice, with the right hashtags and locations
  • Comment-to-DM: someone comments a word, they get your link or offer in their DMs automatically

Best for

Stores & retailFood & simchasHome servicesReal estateBeauty & fashionAnything visual

Our take: for most local businesses in our community, Instagram is the first platform to get right.

LinkedIn

Where owners and professionals do business

For B2B, finance, real estate, law, insurance, staffing and consulting, LinkedIn is where decisions get made. People buy from people, so the owner’s own profile often matters more than the company page.

What we do

  • Founder personal brand: posts written with you, in your voice, about what you know
  • Profile makeover: headline, banner, about section and featured links
  • Company page: set up properly and kept active every week
  • Carousels & insights: useful posts that make you the expert in your field
  • Milestones & hiring: the wins and news that build trust over time

Best for

B2B & servicesFinance & insuranceReal estateLaw & accountingStaffingConsulting

Our take: if your clients are businesses, a steady founder profile is one of the best things you can build.

Facebook

Still where many locals look

Plenty of customers still check a business on Facebook for hours, reviews and events. We keep it accurate and active alongside Instagram, without making it a second job.

What we do

  • Page setup: hours, contact, services and reviews kept correct
  • Posts & events: shared with Instagram content, planned together
  • Community groups: the right local groups for your news and offers
  • Ads: run from the same account when you’re ready (see Advertising)

Best for

Local servicesEventsOlder audiencesRetail

Our take: Facebook rarely leads, but an empty or outdated page costs trust.

WhatsApp

Status and Channels: the community’s feed

In our community a lot of sharing happens on WhatsApp: statuses, groups, family chats, forwarded flyers. We design for it so your news travels the way people already talk.

What we do

  • Status designs & videos: weekly statuses made to be seen and forwarded
  • WhatsApp Channels: a channel for your updates, offers and news
  • Business profile: catalog, hours, greeting and quick replies set up
  • Shareable graphics: flyers and announcements sized for groups and broadcasts

Best for

Every local businessOffers & salesEvents & simchasAnnouncements

X

Where the conversation happens in real time

X is where news, opinions and quick updates move fast. For owners, professionals and brands with something to say, a steady presence there keeps you part of the conversation and easy to find.

What we do

  • Profile setup: name, bio, banner, pinned post and links
  • Posts & threads: short updates and longer threads, written in your voice
  • Visuals & clips: graphics and short videos sized for the timeline
  • Replies & mentions: we keep an eye on them and help you answer
  • Reuse across platforms: your news and content, adapted for X

Best for

Owners & foundersProfessionalsNews & updatesB2BPersonal brands

Our take: X works when there’s a real voice behind it. We help you post regularly, without it taking over your day.

YouTube

YouTube is where people go to watch, learn and search. How-tos, behind the scenes, interviews and Shorts keep working for you long after the day they’re posted.

  • Videos: planned, filmed and edited for your channel
  • Shorts: vertical clips cut from your longer videos

    FAQ

    Good questions.

    Which platforms does my business need?

    It depends on who your clients are. Most local businesses start with Instagram and WhatsApp Status. If you sell to other businesses, LinkedIn matters a lot. We’ll tell you honestly in the first conversation.

    How often will you post?

    Enough to stay consistent without burning out your audience. A typical plan is a few feed posts a week plus stories and statuses, and we adjust as we see what works.

    Do I have to be on camera?

    No. Plenty of great accounts never show the owner. If you’re comfortable, a little of you goes a long way, and we’ll make it easy.

    Do I approve everything first?

    Yes. Every post comes to you in our app before it goes out. Approve it in one tap or leave a comment and we’ll fix it.

    Does anything go out on Shabbos or Yom Tov?

    No. We plan the calendar around Shabbos and Yom Tov, so nothing is posted then.

    Who writes the captions?

    We do, in your voice. You’ll see them with the post when you approve.

    Can you run ads too?

    Yes. Posting builds trust; ads reach new people. We can run both from the same plan. See Advertising.

    How soon will I see results?

    Social media builds over months, not days. Consistency is what compounds. We report every month so you can see what’s working.

    How do we start?

    Send us a WhatsApp. We’ll look at your current pages, ask a few questions and suggest a plan.
