FAQ

Good questions.

Print or online, which is better?

In our community, often both. Print and WhatsApp reach people who aren’t scrolling Instagram; online ads reach people who are. We’ll suggest the mix for your goal.

Do you design for the community magazines?

Yes. We design to each magazine’s exact size and specs and send print-ready files.

How much should I spend on ads?

That’s your call, and it depends on the goal. We’ll recommend a sensible starting point and show you what it did.

Can you run my Instagram and Facebook ads?

Yes. We set up the campaign, the audience and the creative, and keep an eye on it.

Can one idea work across print and social?

That’s the best way to do it. One campaign, adapted for the magazine, the feed, the story and the status.
