FAQ

Good questions.

How long does a brand take?

It depends on the project. Before we start, you get a clear plan with the steps and dates.

What do I get at the end?

Your logo in every format you need (for print, web and social), your colors and fonts, and a simple brand guide so anyone can use it correctly.

Can you refresh my existing logo?

Yes. Many businesses don’t need a new brand, just a cleaner, more modern version of the one people already know.

Will it work on Instagram and WhatsApp?

Yes. We design for the small screen first: profile photos, stories and statuses, not just a big letterhead.

Can you design my signs, cards and packaging too?

Yes. Once the brand is set, we carry it onto everything people see.
