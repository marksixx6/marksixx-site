FAQ

About packages.

How much does it cost?

Marketing starts at $1,000 a month and branding at $2,500 one-time. Prices are on each package above. Every package can be customized to your business, so we confirm the details with you on WhatsApp. No surprises.

Can I start small?

Yes. Start with Presence or one service and add more when it makes sense.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. We work month to month. The first month is the minimum, and each month runs in full, because we plan and make a whole month of content ahead. After your first month, stay because it works, not because you’re locked in. Want to lock 3 months? You save 10%.

Can I change my package later?

Of course. As the business grows, the plan can grow with it.

Which package is right for me?

If you mainly need to show up consistently on social, start with Presence. If you want more content and a face for your brand, Growth. If you want marketing fully off your plate, We Got Your 6. Need a logo or a full brand first? See the Branding tab.

Do I approve everything?

Yes, in every package. Nothing goes out without your OK.
